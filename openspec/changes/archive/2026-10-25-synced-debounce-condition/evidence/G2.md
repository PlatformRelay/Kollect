## Learnings

- tooling: when a test checks that a seeded copy is independent of its source, compare the
  source with a value held outside it; comparing with the variable the source points at is
  tautological under a shallow copy.
- tooling: a multi-pass transition rule asserted as a map of named booleans shows every broken
  pass in one red line.
