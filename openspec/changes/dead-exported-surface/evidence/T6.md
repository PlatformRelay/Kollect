## Learnings

- guidance: `Engine.RegisterTarget` derives its informer context from `e.informerContext()` (engine.go:369) — `context.Background()` unless `Start` ran — never from the caller's ctx. Any test fixture that registers targets must call `engine.Start(ctx)` first, or its ctx-cancel cleanup cannot stop the informers (leak per fixture). The collect fixtures already do it; this task's helper now does too.
