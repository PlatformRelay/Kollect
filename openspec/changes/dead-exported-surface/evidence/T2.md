## Learnings

- tooling: the repo's changelog marks removals of exported names from `api/v1alpha1` as `[**breaking**]` (CHANGELOG.md:19 precedent); the sanctioned form is `type(scope)!:` WITH a `BREAKING CHANGE:` footer (CONTRIBUTING.md:100-104) — `hack/release/cliff.toml` strips the gitmoji token then routes `^[a-z]+(\(.+\))?!:` under "Breaking Changes". A deletion task removing importable-package symbols should use that form from the first commit, not after review.
- tooling: do not run `task changelog` / `bin/git-cliff` locally to verify rendering — the config's `output = "CHANGELOG.md"` makes the preview overwrite the file, and on unpushed commits the GitHub-metadata fetch fails, writing a truncated changelog (cost here: one `git restore CHANGELOG.md`; tree caught dirty only because the close status check looked).
- sensor: a repo-wide `grep . --exclude-dir=.git` for these symbols drowns in the change's own review transcripts (`.err` logs quote the probes); the targeted dirs grep (`docs/ config/ charts/ test/ hack/`) plus the `--include='*.go'` probe is the verifiable pairing.
