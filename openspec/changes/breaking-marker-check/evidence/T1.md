## Green

  revision, an unset `PR_TITLE` and one argument) and the workflow shape all pass.

## git-cliff behaviour

Probed with git-cliff v2.13.1 and `hack/release/cliff.toml` on a scratch repository: a commit with a
plain title and a footer renders `[**breaking**]` with the footer text dropped; a title with two
spaces after the shortcode is skipped as a parse error. The docs and the check text say this.

## Limitations

- The `breaking-marker` job's run on this PR and CI on the head are recorded when the change is
  archived (follow-up PR).
- The verdict is advisory: on `pull_request` a PR controls its own merge ref, so it can edit the
  script. The job is not required.
